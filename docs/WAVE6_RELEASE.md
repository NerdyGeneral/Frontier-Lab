## Deduplication and future training

### What the data represents

Distinct source evidence remains the unit of learning. Authored source profiles also describe repeated processing passes: 10% extra passes for the starter public sample, 20% for specialist packs, and 25% for broad corpus editions. These passes are redundant processing, not extra independent evidence.

After installing the system, cleaning a prepared packet creates a new immutable revision. The original remains available. Cleanup needs `max(1, ceil(rawUnits / 250))` data staff-weeks, and it pauses and resumes through the existing preparation-job controls. The completed receipt captures the source packet, system rollout, distinct units, removed repeated units, previous processing total, and reduction rate.

The new revision preserves distinct units, source identity, quality, domain coverage, broad and behavior evidence, and hosted-use, weight-distribution, and feedback rights. Curation can carry a completed cleanup receipt forward while paying its own work and preserving lineage.

### Work savings and conservation

Future training update work receives a weighted reduction based on the cleaned portion of its selected corpus, capped at **10%**. The starter sample has 100 distinct units and 10 extra processing passes, yielding **9.09%** after fixed-point rounding. Its reference update budget changes from **12 to 10.9092 CUw**, saving **1.0908 CUw**. Specialist and broad sources reach the 10% cap when fully eligible.

The saving composes after efficient adaptation. Teacher inference, checkpoint overhead, evaluations, memory, model targets, and model outcomes keep their separate rules. A run captures its requirement and cleanup receipt when admitted; a later cleanup never rewrites that run's budget.

The original and cleaned revision share source accounting, corpus fingerprint, and training identity. Selecting both cannot double evidence or create another result roll. A weaker cleaned revision also cannot supply a saving for a stronger dirty revision selected from that source. Forged rights, receipts, work, methods, and lineage are rejected on import.

## Document evidence checking

### Application and metering

R6A applies to the generic **Business Documents** subscription product with its own active retrieval index. A checker captures the serving model and retrieval source. Replacing either invalidates that implementation and requires a new rollout. Its operating toggle applies at the next weekly boundary.

Each enabled request adds **0.00002 CUw** after model, routing, and prefix-cache work adjustments. The extra work participates in normal serving grants, headroom, physical work, owned-GPU costs, and fulfillment. Retrieval lookup, source upkeep, index memory, sessions, and model inference remain separate requirements. There is no second charge for the same work.

The settled usage receipt records requested, fully checked, supported, withheld, and unfinished request counts, plus actual checking work. Only a complete request bundle can create a completed check. A partially executed request can consume compute but cannot create completed evidence. Trial checks remain free to the customer while using company resources. An idle checker creates no usage or revenue.

Modeled support is the installed source's quality multiplied by document coverage and actual upkeep freshness. Those inputs are captured in the usage record. Historical meters reconcile against their captured inputs and request cost; the current week additionally reconciles with the active implementation and actual upkeep.

### Behavior, billing, trust, and retention

Unsupported outputs become withholding or clarification. This reduces the modeled incidence of confidently wrong emitted outputs, while leaving the model weights and technical execution-error measure unchanged. A citation supported by the index can still be factually wrong; source support is not proof of truth.

Business subscriptions continue billing fulfilled attempts, including clarifications, under their existing terms. Unsupported answers do not earn a separate success fee. Withholding adds up to **6 percentage points of customer loss at the existing business renewal boundary**, scaled by the withheld share. This is not a weekly 6% churn charge.

An evidence-enabled paid product must also support at least **80% of completed checks** to qualify for a good company-trust week, alongside the existing fulfillment and technical-reliability checks. No completed checks cannot satisfy that additional requirement. The company still receives at most one combined trust result per week.

Named private deployments and customer-hosted licenses retain their captured obligations and billing. A customer-hosted license still uses the customer's GPUs and requires the supplier's delivery and support effort; the generic document checker does not silently attach inference work to that license.

## Twelve-week capacity planning

The installed planner compares **base** and **heavier-load** assumptions, defaulting to 2% and 5% net paid-account growth per week. Inputs accept 0–10%, with the heavier case at least as large as the base case. The first row uses accounts already due next week; later rows compound the chosen assumption within the finite current market. A product with zero accounts gains none from this calculation.

The planner uses the same demand, workforce-grant, compute-grant, compatible-pool, and rental-admission rules as the simulation. It shows:

- Paid accounts and repeated current trial load.
- Public-product work and captured private-service promises.
- Current physical grants, serving headroom, and the weakest workload's capacity fit.
- Required versus granted memory in one compatible connected pool.
- Recurring data, commercial, and infrastructure staff commitments.
- The first constrained week and the affected product or staff role.
- A proposed whole-CU cloud reservation, serving allocation, and incremental weekly rent.

All signed private deployments and licenses remain reserved throughout the window, including delivery-stage promises. Continuing beyond a term boundary is a planning assumption, not a guaranteed renewal. The current ready workforce and commissioned hardware are held fixed; queued actions, onboarding, equipment deliveries, and data-center construction do not create forecast capacity.

The cloud comparison first considers unallocated existing capacity, then searches permitted whole-CU cloud additions through the real allocator. It preserves development and research allocations. General cloud cannot provide missing staff, sparse compatibility, or fast fabric. Unpaid bills leave owed demand visible while usable grants remain suspended.

This is a customer-service plan. Transient agent jobs and research-assistant calls need additional serving capacity beyond the displayed load. Acquisition, churn, outages, future upgrades, and competition are not predicted. Revenue, capital spending, construction payments, and future cash balances are not forecast. The planner mutates no company state, cash, reservations, history, or random streams; the player applies rental, allocation, and hiring decisions separately.

A failed archive blocks replacement; a failed primary write leaves the old primary and any successful archive available.
