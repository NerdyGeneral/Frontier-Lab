## Deliberate scope and remaining work

This wave completes the first commercial loop. It supports one compact dense model recipe, one reusable starter dataset, one twenty-case conversation evaluation, one Basic Chat offer, one reference consumer segment, and rented general-purpose compute. The evaluation is useful evidence for the opening product gate; it is not a general alignment or safety certification.

The founder can cover opening roles; an employee hire is a strategic choice rather than an artificial mandatory gate. Infrastructure staff remain preparatory for later facility operations. Hardware specialists are gated. Research execution, more datasets and recipes, detailed alignment/model behavior, rivals, owned hardware and data centers, agent products, licenses, and custom chips remain later-wave work.

Saves remain browser-local with portable exports. There is one active save plus recovery copies, no cloud synchronization or multi-slot campaign manager, and no offline catch-up. Balance is verified for the opening loop and fixed scenarios, not a completed long campaign. All costs and AI scores are fictional simulation values.
