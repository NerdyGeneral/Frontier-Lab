# Wave 4 — Late game

Frontier Lab **0.5.0**, snapshot schema **5**, connects applied research, specialized hardware, and campaign ambitions to the existing deterministic company simulation. Wave 3's agent, contract, retrieval, licensing, and save-recovery systems remain active. All models, customers, money, and hardware are fictional game records.

## Design rules

1. Research opens an application. It does not silently improve existing weights, install a system, or deliver hardware.
2. Model derivation captures its source, evidence, rights, and targets. Repeating a command cannot reroll a result or compound a finite improvement.
3. Training, teacher inference, serving, implementation, and research use the shared resource allocator. Actual physical GPU work and effective task work remain separate.
4. Architecture, workload, connected memory, fabric, staff, power, and cooling determine compatible capacity. A nominal CU number alone is insufficient.
5. An ending requires consecutive settled evidence, including paid service and sustainable finances. A model held in inventory or an unserved account is insufficient.

### Deployment derivatives

Compression and memory format create immutable versions in the model registry. The source's capability evaluation is not copied to the derivative. A fresh evaluation is required before release, and choosing a variant for inspection does not change an existing deployment. The two formats are alternatives: variants cannot be stacked or retrained to erase their saved penalty. Future training starts from source weights.

The model application stores its own required/completed ML effort, effective work, physical work, status, and output version. Pause/resume uses the existing job command and preserves completed work. Sources with existing applications cannot manufacture duplicate versions by repeating the command.

### Routing and caching

These are installations on generic public products: Basic Chat, Developer API, and Business Documents. Named private contracts keep their captured deployment obligations. Routing requires an evaluated, commercially eligible, cheaper secondary with the same architecture. The two resident models must fit a connected pool. Effective capability, reliability, and behavior reflect the traffic mixture; efficiency does not erase a weaker secondary's quality tradeoff.

An installation captures the primary model. Replacing that product's primary makes the old installation inactive until it is rebuilt for the new version. Choosing a different secondary also takes a fresh rollout. The primary continues to serve while implementation is incomplete. An installed method can be disabled or re-enabled without granting new capability.

Prefix caching permits reuse for half the input workload. A warm hit uses 20% of that input's ordinary work; output and internal reasoning remain fully charged. The cache is scoped to the company, product, selected releases, features, and retrieval evidence. It reserves a base memory allocation plus per-customer memory. Actual served traffic warms it; changes invalidate it, and entries expire after two weeks without reuse. API token bills continue to reflect executed tokens rather than treating compute savings as unearned tokens.

### Distillation and sparse models

Distillation requires a held-out evaluated owned teacher with inherited weight-transfer rights. The selected domain needs a score of at least 60 and a ten-point lead over the student's saved lineage anchor. Hosted access alone does not authorize transfer. The student's final rights follow both lineages. The captured teacher requires separate serving work and memory before the student receives development work. Repeating the same identity cannot produce a new roll or cumulative target improvement.

Sparse foundation designs retain 70% of dense update work, use 65% of dense serving work, and require 150% of the resident-weight memory. Their training, evaluation, and inference requests carry architecture and fast-fabric requirements. Research alone does not supply a usable sparse pool. Adaptation and behavior recipes preserve the saved architecture; a compact distillation student remains dense.

### Supervision and research assistance

The installed supervisor changes eligible training supervision from a flat work-per-ML-FTE cap to a requirement of 0.5 ML FTE plus 0.25 per started eight-CU development allocation group, with another 0.5 FTE for an unfamiliar family/architecture until an evaluated completed run exists. Partial supervision scales the achievable throughput. The ceiling is still derived from compatible, memory-fitting physical pools and their hardware/fabric efficiency.

Research assistance requires held-out coding 65 and tool use 60. The player can enable a qualified model and set 0.25–4 assisted FTE. Each assisted FTE has a baseline cost of 0.25 serving CUw, adjusted by the model's execution format, and 0.10 commercial review FTE. Only actual research-role staff grants are eligible for up to 20% extra progress. Assistance cannot replace research compute, assist its own method research, or recursively boost its own bonus. The weekly view reports requested/executed/physical work, review, staff assisted, and actual bonus separately.

## Custom chips and networking

### Entry and staffing

R16 — Chip feasibility requires Compression, Enterprise operations, and eight distinct weeks of actual customer-serving work. Idle allocations and internal assistance do not qualify as customer service. Starting the feasibility project enables hardware-specialist hiring; it does not require completing an impossible project before hiring its staff. The founder continues to cover the five general roles, while chip engineering uses specialized hires.

The serving record is bounded to eight distinct recorded weeks. A migrated save begins collecting this new evidence through future ordinary operation rather than receiving invented historical work.

### Specification and prototype

A custom design selects dense or sparse architecture, inference or training workload, and balanced or wide memory. These choices capture efficiency, memory per useful CU, power, variable cost, engineering effort, and production lead time. Wide memory trades throughput and price for a larger working set. Inference hardware and training hardware are specialized; unsupported work cannot use their capacity.

Design fees are separate from hardware staff effort and research simulation. A finished specification then needs a paid prototype stage using an owned evaluated model of the same architecture, hardware staff, ML validation, and development compute. Completion reveals a deterministic qualified yield. The specification and campaign seed determine that yield; renaming or ordering again cannot reroll it.

### Fabrication and operation

Only a qualified design can place a production order. Batches occupy one, two, or four rack slots, with price based on attempted capacity and useful capacity based on yield. The manufacturing delay is five weeks plus sparse/wide complexity. Delivery creates rack inventory; it does not grant installed capacity. The existing installation and park controls operate the custom racks.

Every rack continues to need connected memory, operations staff, facility slots, power, cooling, fixed upkeep, and variable charges for actual physical work. Lower yield reduces useful compute while retaining the reserved rack footprint. Batch, design, and rack counts are bounded; orders and deliveries retain durable payment and identity records.

Fast fabric is a separate installation at colocation or one owned site. Its three elapsed weeks, two infrastructure staff-weeks, and four development CUw must all finish before activation. It then carries its own fixed upkeep and operations-staff requirement. It improves cross-rack training and permits sparse work in compatible pools, while leaving separate sites, hardware classes, and cloud memory separate.

### Payback estimate

The production panel calls the shared `chipPaybackForecast` helper. The player selects batch size, destination, and assumed sustained utilization. The view exposes useful capacity, effective work, design/production estimate, equivalent reserved-cloud cost, variable physical-work cost, fixed costs and staffing, weekly savings, and simple payback.

The estimate lists its assumptions and exclusions. It is not a revenue forecast or capacity reservation. Before qualification it uses a stated 75% planning yield; afterward it uses the saved qualified yield. Site construction, onboarding, research fees, simulation cloud rent, installation wages, and lead-time carrying costs are excluded as stated in the helper. A nonpositive weekly saving produces no payback result rather than a misleading negative duration.

## Campaign ambitions

Three routes share operating health requirements and use actual paid delivered service. Each route's live view lists the exact current value, target, and explanation for every criterion.

| Route | Required streak | Strategic focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frontier laboratory | Eight consecutive weeks | A trained large or sparse model delivering paid service, at least 80 in every capability domain, reliability 92, and a behavior holdout with at most five confident errors per 100 cases. |
| Trusted platform | Twelve consecutive weeks | At least 600 paying consumer accounts and 60 developer accounts across two product families, with trust at least 75. |
| Essential specialist | Twelve consecutive weeks | At least 12 served business organizations, a trained domain model scoring at least 65 with reliability 92, and a named commitment with at least four delivered weeks meeting its term service target. |

The routes also have distinct revenue, margin, and service floors. All require eight current base-cost weeks of cash and no arrears. An audited agent penalty interrupts qualification. The frontier route requires $10,000 weekly earned revenue, a nonnegative operating margin, and 95% paid service. Platform requires $6,000, a 15% margin, and 98% service. Specialist requires $3,000, a 10% margin, and 98% service.

The campaign stores the latest sixteen settled weeks and a fixed proof window for each earned ending. Missing a requirement resets an unfinished streak. Replaying the same week cannot advance it; changing an already recorded week's evidence is invalid. Starting scenario model scores do not stand in for trained frontier or specialist models. Once earned, an ending remains recorded while the company continues to operate and pursue other routes.
