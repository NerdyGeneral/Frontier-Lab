# Wave 11 — Policy learning

Content/build **0.12.0**, save schema **5**. This wave extends the existing Frontier Lab site and keeps the text-first, Universal Paperclips-inspired interface.

## What is playable

The new loop is **licensed policy pairs → paid training → exact-candidate capability evidence → independent policy holdout → paid rehearsal and chosen release → ordinary customer use and feedback**.

Policy learning makes a distinction the original behavior suite could not measure: whether the model refuses a legitimate request or complies with a restricted one. The existing suite remains unchanged for historical saves and reproducibility. Its description now identifies its 90 benign cases and ten authored refusal controls; those controls do not establish policy robustness.

These probabilities, prices, cases and thresholds are authored game rules. They are not measurements of real AI models or real-world safety guarantees.

### Dedicated data

| Property | Rule |
|---|---|
| Catalog item | Policy contrast pairs, edition 1 |
| Purchase | $8,000, charged once |
| Preparation | Three actual data staff-weeks |
| Volume | 100 matched allowed/restricted pairs; 200 individual cases |
| Starting quality | 90% |
| Rights | Hosted use and weight distribution; no customer-feedback reuse |
| Ordinary training coverage | Zero domain, broad and generic behavior units |
| Curation | Three data staff-weeks create a 95%-quality revision of the same source |

The dataset is acquired and prepared through Data. It does not become usable merely by being purchased. Its readiness must reconcile to the accepted preparation command and the unique completed staff project. Curated policy revisions have the corresponding source and paid-work proofs.

Original and curated copies contribute the same 100 pairs once. A higher-quality revision replaces the lower-quality version for evidence quality without increasing volume. Generic specialist packs and customer-feedback datasets do not supply policy labels. Conversely, policy labels do not supply free capability, reliability or foundation evidence. Generic-text deduplication cannot discount the paired-policy update work.

### Two training choices

Both recipes require **Preference tuning (R5)** and **Behavior cases (R1A)**. They use the ordinary Lab queue, ML coverage, connected training workspace, actual GPU grants, and checkpoint timing. The fixed costs below are normalized update work; actual physical work follows the hardware that receives the job.

| Choice | Update work | Absolute restricted-compliance target | Absolute false-refusal target | Full result using the 90%-quality pack |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Policy boundary discrimination | 24 CUw | 6% | 3.5% | 6.60% restricted compliance; 3.85% false refusals |
| Conservative policy refusal | 16 CUw | 3% | 12% | 3.90% restricted compliance; 11.50% false refusals |

The authored restricted-compliance anchor is 12%; the ordinary lineage's original false-refusal anchor is 7%. Progress combines earned work, distinct paired evidence and its quality. The game computes each recipe from these fixed anchors. Alternating methods selects another tradeoff and cannot retain both methods' best values or compound toward perfection. Repeating an already completed lineage/recipe/evidence identity cannot reroll it.

With the curated 95%-quality pack, discrimination yields 6.30% restricted compliance and 3.67% false refusals. The UI previews the selected packet's actual finite result before starting work.

Policy training preserves task capabilities, reliability, confident-error tendency, planner behavior, architecture, residency and per-request inference work. The new candidate carries an optional policy trait; it also changes the established false-refusal property used by serving. Ordinary descendant training and deployment variants preserve policy. A fresh foundation resets policy to the new-foundation baseline, as it resets other inherited learned behavior.

### Separate policy evidence

**Policy boundary holdouts** costs **4 development CUw and one research staff-week**. It requires R1A and a previously completed paid capability report for that exact candidate. The policy suite is a diagnostic and never supplies capability eligibility or modifies weights.

Its 100 reserved cases contain:

- **50 allowed requests**, reported as useful answers, confident errors, unnecessary refusals and clarifications.
- **50 restricted requests**, reported as correct refusals or restricted compliances.
- Within each class, **25 direct and 25 adversarial cases**. Authored pressure multipliers are 0.75× and 1.25× the restricted-compliance tendency, preserving its mean across the equal groups.

Case categories are abstract privacy, permissions, harmful assistance and legitimate sensitive requests. The game contains no operational harmful prompt instructions.

Cases are reserved independently from the purchased training pairs. The cohort is fixed by seed, company and protocol; candidate IDs, display names, command IDs, timing and the simulation RNG cannot select easier cases. Admission freezes the exact candidate fingerprint and its paid capability baseline. Reports reconstruct every case and both denominators from that admission. The UI exposes these separate counts instead of an aggregate alignment score.

### Commercial use requires the exact report

Models that adopt policy training need their own paid policy report with **at most five restricted compliances among the 50 restricted cases**. This applies to new public deployments and replacements, request-routing secondaries, release rehearsals, prepared releases, private-hosted contracts, customer-hosted weight licenses and agent orders.

A runtime variant inherits the learned properties but must earn its own capability and policy reports. A report on the source model cannot authorize the changed artifact. Missing, copied, failed or postdated evidence cannot satisfy admission. Saved commercial histories also retain and validate the evidence timing, so removing a report or backdating a commercial action cannot make a valid snapshot.

The policy check supplements existing task qualifications, rights, memory, staffing and capacity requirements. Licensed policy data cannot erase a hosted-only ancestor's prohibition on weight distribution. A policy report alone does not approve agent actions, expand tool permissions, or qualify general task capability.

Legacy models retain their existing eligibility until this policy method is adopted. Research, dataset purchase, training and testing do not change a live deployment. Release preparation and explicit promotion remain separate player choices.

### Usage and costs remain connected

The changed false-refusal property already feeds public product behavior, routing mixtures, customer friction, feedback sampling, agent drafts and model rehearsals. Conservative refusal therefore has an economic cost: more legitimate help is withheld, with higher retention friction. Discrimination restores some useful answers without inventing new technical capability.

Weight training introduces no extra per-request filtering call. Training, evaluation and rehearsal consume their actual granted work and normal staff costs. Serving continues to pay the ordinary GPU, rent, power, site and payroll bills; customer billing tokens and fractional carries retain their current rules. Runtime moderation, live harmful-request incidents and universal safety certification are outside this wave.

The full progression verifies that the old invoice has left normal history, its payment proof remains, and the original study hash is unchanged. Old-engine oracle weeks before that expiry boundary remain identical.

## Deliberate remaining scope

This wave models a limited learned boundary and its refusal tradeoff. It does not model autonomous deceptive goals, strategic evaluation gaming, sycophancy, general alignment, production moderation calls, policy-specific live incidents or a policy warranty for enterprise customers. Agent permissions and verified outcomes continue to use their existing independent rules.

A future runtime guardrail would be a separate operational choice with recurring checking work, resident memory, installation effort, routing coverage, changed product configuration and preserved billing semantics. It should also distinguish justified policy refusals from ordinary unhappy-customer feedback before those samples can become policy training data.
